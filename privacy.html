Privacy Policy for Voxel Rush

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Z Sphere ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in the mobile game Voxel Rush.

Voxel Rush is an entertainment game that can be played offline, but includes optional online features described below (advertising and in-app purchases).

2. Information We Collect Directly

Voxel Rush itself does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, or other personal information from you.

3. Local Game Data

Voxel Rush uses standard device storage, including localStorage, to save game progress, settings, achievements, coins, and other game-related information.

This information is stored locally on your device. Users can remove this locally stored game data by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the game.

4. Advertising

Voxel Rush displays advertisements, including banner ads and optional rewarded video ads, served through Google AdMob. To provide and measure these ads, AdMob and its partner networks may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general device information, and app interaction data, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

You can review Google's practices here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Where required by applicable law (such as in the EEA, UK, or California), you will be shown a consent option before personalized ads are displayed.

5. In-App Purchases

Voxel Rush offers optional in-app purchases (such as coin packs) processed entirely through the Google Play billing system. We do not receive or store your payment card details - these are handled directly by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Limited technical data may be shared with Google AdMob and Google Play strictly as described in Sections 4 and 5, for the purpose of displaying ads and processing purchases.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress data is stored locally on your device and is not retained by Z Sphere. Data collected by AdMob or Google Play is retained and controlled according to Google's own policies, linked above.

You may delete your locally stored game data at any time by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the game.

8. Children's Privacy

Voxel Rush is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising shown in the app is not configured for child-directed treatment.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
