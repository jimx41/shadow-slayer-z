Privacy Policy for Shadow Slayer Z

Last updated: August 16, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Z Studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in the mobile game Shadow Slayer Z.

Shadow Slayer Z is an offline game intended for entertainment purposes.

2. Information We Collect

Shadow Slayer Z does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The game does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, or other personal information.

3. Local Game Data

Shadow Slayer Z may use standard device storage, including localStorage, to save game progress, settings, achievements, and other game-related information.

This information is stored locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to Z Studio or to third parties.

Users can remove this locally stored game data by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the game.

4. Internet and Third-Party Services

Shadow Slayer Z does not require an internet connection to play and does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or other online services to collect or transmit user information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose user information to third parties because Shadow Slayer Z does not collect personal information.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because game data is stored locally on the user's device, Z Studio does not retain a copy of this data.

Users may delete their locally stored game data at any time by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the game.

7. Children's Privacy

Shadow Slayer Z does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
